Recurring meeting & notes (Google Meet)

Set up the meeting once in Google Calendar, then keep the Meet link and the notes doc here so they are one click away every week.

New notes doc
How to get the notes saved every week
  1. Click Create recurring event in Google Calendar. The event opens with your day, time and repeat already filled in. Click Add Google Meet video conferencing, add your guests, and save. Choose "all events" if Calendar asks.
  2. Open the saved event, copy the Meet link, and paste it above. That one link works for every repeat of the meeting.
  3. Notes doc: in the event, use Create meeting notes (next to attachments) if you see it. It attaches a Google Doc to the event and shares it with guests when you save. If you do not see it, click New notes doc, then attach it with the paperclip and paste its link above.
  4. Automatic notes: if your Google account has Meet's Take notes for me (it needs an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan), turn it on in the meeting. The notes are saved to the organizer's Drive in a Google Meet folder, attached to the Calendar event, and emailed to the organizer. Hosts can keep it on for future repeats of a recurring meeting.
  5. After each meeting, click Log this week in Meeting Notes to save the decisions and action items here with the doc link.
