The Program

What is The AI Lab?

The Studio

The Brown Girls Creative Studio

The Brown Girls Creative Studio builds data systems, websites, dashboards, AI solutions, automation, and digital growth infrastructure for businesses and organizations[cite: 9].

The Lab

Where students become builders.

The AI Lab brings those same creative and technical skills into student-centered learning experiences. Students practice AI literacy, prompting, web creation, teamwork, problem-solving, entrepreneurship, and presentation skills through hands-on projects[cite: 6, 7, 8, 9, 13].

Real Skills

What students learn.

The goal is simple: students spend their time thinking like AI designers and creators — not fighting with code.

💬

AI Literacy

Understand what AI can do, how to use it responsibly, and how to communicate with it effectively.

🧠

Prompt Engineering

Learn how clearer instructions produce stronger, more useful AI results.

💻

Digital Creation

Turn ideas into websites, landing pages, prototypes, and other digital experiences.

🎨

Creative Problem-Solving

Explore ideas, audiences, branding, user experience, and ways technology can solve real problems.

🤝

Team Collaboration

Work together, divide responsibilities, communicate, and turn a group idea into a finished concept[cite: 6].

🎤

Pitching & Communication

Explain what was built, who it helps, why it matters, and how the idea could grow[cite: 7].

The Experience

How an AI Lab project comes to life.

01

Imagine

Choose an idea, audience, problem, or opportunity.

02

Prompt

Learn how to give AI clear instructions and improve results through iteration.

03

Build

Turn the idea into a real digital project, website, prototype, or experience.

04

Pitch

Present the idea, explain the value, and show what was created[cite: 7].
