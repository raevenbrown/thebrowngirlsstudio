How Vibe City works

🧑🏾‍🏫Your teacher guides you

Mrs. Brown teaches you to prompt: to tell an AI exactly what to build, in detail, step by step.

🤖Your agent is a practice builder

It follows rules and builds from the words you type. It is not a real AI, so it only knows what you tell it. That is the lesson: more detail, better build.

✨Real AI shows up live

Mrs. Brown can run a real AI on the big screen so you can compare. A real AI is smarter, but it can still be wrong, so we always test.

🔒Private by design

No accounts. Use initials and made-up names only. Never type your full name, school, phone, address or anyone’s email. Nothing you type leaves this Chromebook.
